Preventive Law: Protect Your Company from Risks

Corporate 6 min read Last updated: October 15, 2025

Winning a lawsuit is a success, but ensuring that no lawsuit is filed at all is a much greater success. "Preventive law" is to commercial life what "preventive medicine" is to medicine.

TL;DR

Preventive law prevents legal problems before they arise through contract review and risk analysis. Regular legal consultancy protects companies in the Cerkezkoy industrial zone from employment and commercial law risks.

Why Should You Get Legal Consultancy?

Companies operating in industrial zones such as Cerkezkoy and Kapakli face many legal risks in their daily operations. A small negligence can turn into large compensations in the future.

Our preventive law service covers the following main areas:

Contract Management

Preparation of supplier, customer, and employee contracts. Regulation of "penalty clauses", "authorized court", and "termination" clauses in favor of the company.

Labor Law Compliance

Keeping employee personnel files complete, taking defenses and minutes in accordance with the procedure, compliance with occupational safety legislation. In this way, the success rate in labor lawsuits increases.

KVKK Compliance

Preparation of VERBIS registration and clarification texts within the scope of the Personal Data Protection Law. Otherwise, administrative fines of millions of liras are in question.

Mistakes Made When Preparing Contracts

"Copy-paste" draft contracts found on the internet are the biggest enemies of companies. Every commercial relationship is unique.

A good contract should be prepared by considering the worst-case scenario. For example, notification periods in case the goods are defective or who will bear the damages arising from exchange rate differences should be clear.

Cost Analysis

The annual legal consultancy fee is much lower than a single lost severance pay lawsuit or a penalty clause paid due to a faulty contract.

Legal consultancy is not an expense, but an insurance that protects your company.

What is preventive law?

Preventive law is an approach to identifying and preventing legal problems before they arise. Contract review, risk analysis, and legal compliance auditing fall within this scope.

Why should companies get legal consultancy?

Regular legal consultancy protects companies from potential lawsuits, administrative penalties, and contract violations. Especially for firms in the Cerkezkoy industrial zone, employment and commercial law risks are high.

What does contract review service cover?

Contract review includes risk analysis, legal compliance check, evaluation of parties' rights and obligations, penalty clause review, and revision recommendations.
