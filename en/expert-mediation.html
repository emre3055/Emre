Instead of waiting years in court hallways; choose mediation for a fast, cost-effective solution under the parties' control.

Atty. Fatih Özden is an officially registered mediator with the Turkish Ministry of Justice Mediation Department. Developed as an alternative to traditional litigation, mediation is a confidential and amicable method where parties can create their own solutions. We serve as a bridge in disputes for businesses and individuals in Cerkezkoy.

Our Service Areas

  • Employment Law (Mandatory): Mandatory mediation process for severance pay, notice pay, overtime, and wrongful termination claims.
  • Commercial Mediation: Confidential resolution for inter-company receivables, contract breaches, and compensation disputes.
  • Rental & Real Estate: Mandatory mediation for tenant eviction, rent determination, and condominium disputes.
  • Consumer Disputes: Defective goods and services, banking transaction issues.
  • Voluntary Mediation: Any private law dispute that parties can freely dispose of.

Important Note: Settlement agreements reached through mediation have the force of court judgments and are directly enforceable.
