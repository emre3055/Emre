Our Practice Areas

  • Title Deed Cancellation & Registration: Lawsuits filed due to error, fraud, intimidation, or inheritance collusion.
  • Rental Law: Eviction due to necessity, rent determination, 10-year extension period, and eviction commitment lawsuits.
  • Partition Lawsuits (Izale-i Suyu): Sharing of properties under joint ownership through sale or physical division.
  • Expropriation Cases: Price increase and compensation lawsuits in case of confiscation by the administration.
