Administrative Law Attorney in Cerkezkoy

We stand by citizens in legal problems with government institutions, municipalities, and administrative authorities.

Administrative law is a field that protects individuals' rights against actions and acts performed by the state using its public power. We represent our clients in Tekirdag Administrative Courts for the annulment of unlawful administrative actions and compensation for damages caused by the administration's actions.

Our Practice Areas

  • Annulment Lawsuits: Cancellation of administrative actions such as appointment, disciplinary penalty, license revocation due to unlawfulness.
  • Full Remedy Lawsuits: Compensation for material/moral damages suffered by citizens due to the administration's service fault or strict liability.
  • Zoning Law: Zoning plan cancellations, illegal construction fines, and objection processes against demolition decisions.
  • Civil Servant Lawsuits: Disputes regarding discipline, promotion, appointment, and personal rights of public officials.

Deadlines Are Critical

In administrative lawsuits, filing deadlines (usually 60 days) are non-extendable. Do not waste time.
